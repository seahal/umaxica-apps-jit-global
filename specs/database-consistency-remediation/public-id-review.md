# Public-ID shared contract review

Status: investigation; no capacity or validator change applied. The subsequent actual-CHECK
inventory in `public-id-check-review.md` corrects an important omission: all 109 Publishing
public_id targets also require exactly 21 characters in the DB, including MediaFile. Varchar
capacity widening alone is insufficient and is not authorized by the shared capacity ADR.

The shared Concern applies to 264 concrete table-backed models in this runtime. All are listed
below, not merely representative cases. Of the 216 displayed length failures, 197 have only the
shared maximum 25; nine also have maximum 21, and ten also require exactly 21. The checker selects
the first length validator and therefore misses the effective stricter constraint on these 19
models. Reordering declarations to silence that limitation is not a valid fix. Their independent
capacity/semantic ADR assessment remains open. Current tests and consumers must guide any change; no
concrete 21-character validator is removed, and generation remains 21.

The identifier ADR calls for standard capacity 25. The physical varchar(21) ceiling needs a
per-owner expansion/lock/recovery design rather than reducing the shared capacity to 21. Exact 21
occurrence identities and token/device/grant contracts need separate compatibility assessment.
Maximum lengths below are read-only local aggregates and say nothing about production data.
`Consumer and boundary tests pending` means the row is inventoried, not behaviorally verified. The
existing generator/capacity tests protect only their stated classified scope.
