# Accessibility review prompts

Use the applicable accessibility standard for exact thresholds; these prompts do not claim formal
conformance. Exercise the relevant interaction with keyboard and assistive technology where available.

- Do controls expose an accurate name, role, value and state?
- Are labels, instructions and errors associated with their fields and announced appropriately?
- Does focus remain visible and predictable, including entering and leaving dialogs?
- Can the flow be completed without a pointer, without a keyboard trap?
- Does content remain usable at narrow widths and zoom, including error and loading states?
- Are meaning and status conveyed without relying only on color or motion?
- Do motion settings, media alternatives and semantic structure support the intended users?

Prefer native semantics and existing components. Report manual checks separately from automated
results; a passing automated audit does not establish complete accessibility.
