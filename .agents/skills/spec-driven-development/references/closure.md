# Close an accepted change

Account for each acceptance criterion using relevant tests, observations and evidence. A failed or
materially unverified criterion remains open unless the owner explicitly changes scope. Qualify
revision and environment; a local pass is not a deployment claim.

Promote lasting conditions to durable checklists, current explanation to docs, and accepted design
decisions to ADRs. Keep one-time migration acceptance and rejected alternatives with their historical
change. Close only after those dispositions are recorded. Corrections preserve the meaning of closed
specs; later contract changes use a linked successor spec. Evidence corrections use a new record.
