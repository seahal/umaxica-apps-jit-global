## Root cause of each original failure

1. `HostAuthorizationContractTest` (2 failures): the development boot now eagerly resolves the
   `:avatar` and `:publishing` storage boundaries, which requires a complete S3-compatible
   configuration. The test's environment-clearing isolation predates boundary registration and left
   the child unable to boot. Fixed by supplying a complete test-local object-storage configuration.
2. `PromoteRevisionRaceVerificationTest` (1 failure): the test stubbed methods on an ActiveRecord
   relation captured before `PromoteRevisionOperation#call`, but `entry.with_lock` reloads the
   record and drops that relation, so the operation never saw the stub and the "other unique index
   must propagate" case was not exercised. Fixed by moving the stub onto the record and hardening
   the operation's constraint identification.
3. `RetainableTest#test_validation_debug_log_fires_...` (1 failure, introduced mid-audit): removing
   a stray `DEBUG:` log line broke a test that existed only to assert that line. Replaced with a
   test of the real validation contract.
