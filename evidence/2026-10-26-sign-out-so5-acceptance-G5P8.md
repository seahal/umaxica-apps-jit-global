## Owner manual browser check — to be done by the owner

Per realm, with two RPs signed in in one browser:

1. Sign out at RP 1 and confirm at Visa: RP 1 completion appears once, reload is 404, and RP 2 asks for sign-in.
2. Sign out at RP 1 and cancel at Visa: RP 1 is signed out and RP 2 remains signed in.
3. After step 1, go back to the old confirmation and confirm again: already signed out, nothing else changes.
4. Open Visa `/oidc/logout` directly while signed in and confirm: the OP signed-out page appears once.
5. Open it while signed out: the already-signed-out page appears.
6. Sign in on APP and COM, sign out on APP, and confirm COM remains signed in.
