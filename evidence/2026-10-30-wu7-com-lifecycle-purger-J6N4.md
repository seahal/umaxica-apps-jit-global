- HEAD: `336811e5072a93c0523e484f905082635b28f48f`; staged, unstaged and untracked changes affected the result.
- Added `PreferenceLifecycleJob` and `PreferenceLifecyclePurger` for com. Candidate selection uses the com setting DB clock and last-issued expiry plus 30 days, in batches of 200. For each candidate the purger acquires the exclusive Chronicle advisory transaction lock before locking the setting parent, rechecks the setting DB clock and checks Chronicle subject references. It destroys the loaded children and parent within one setting transaction only if no audit reference exists. The existing retention suspension flag stops the job. A search of the current com models and migrations found no com-specific hold model; this is not evidence about external or future hold systems.
- Model-based creation or reassignment of a com Chronicle subject now acquires the shared audit lock and verifies the setting parent exists. Direct SQL or validation/callback-bypassing bulk operations do not participate in this coordination. The job is not registered in a recurring schedule; no non-test purge was executed.
