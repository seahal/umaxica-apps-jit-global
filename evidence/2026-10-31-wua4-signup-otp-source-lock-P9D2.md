# Signup OTP Source and Ticket lock order

- HEAD: `336811e5072a93c0523e484f905082635b28f48f`; uncommitted worktree changes affected the result.
- The APP Email and Telephone signup OTP issuers, delivery guards, verification committers and post-verification reset paths now acquire the Source owner and Contact locks before the Ticket flow and ceremony locks. They retain the existing locked owner, Contact, nonce, parent, deadline and generation checks.
- Before the change, an independent Email test held the owner and found that a delivery waiting for it had already locked the flow (`PG::LockNotAvailable` on `FOR UPDATE NOWAIT`; 1 run, 2 assertions, 1 error). Afterward, independent tests for both channels observed all three paths waiting on the owner while the flow remained lockable. Existing cancellation and resend races were adapted to observe the actual Source or Ticket connection and to pause the real operation without creating an extra outer Ticket transaction.
- An expanded intermediate run produced three lock-timeout errors in tests that still assumed the old lock position; after adapting those tests the ordering cases passed. A Telephone failure-reset test injected its second failure after taking the Contact lock and failed under the new order; it now injects before that lock, preserving its intended interleaving. A scoped read of synthetic Email candidates created during the intermediate error run found none remaining.
- After formatting, `PARALLEL_WORKERS=1 bin/rails test test/operations/email_sign_up_otp_issuer_test.rb test/operations/telephone_sign_up_otp_issuer_test.rb test/operations/sign_up_otp_delivery_guard_test.rb test/operations/sign_up_otp_delivery_concurrency_test.rb test/services/signup_otp_committed_retry_test.rb test/notifiers/notify/email_sign_up_otp_delivery_test.rb test/integration/telephone_sign_up_otp_delivery_test.rb` passed: 64 runs, 729 assertions, no failures or errors. Scoped RuboCop passed on six operations and two changed tests.
- The races use local transport blocks, not actual SMTP or SNS. This does not establish all other signup writers' lock ordering, behavior under pre-existing outer transactions, or the complete two.md issuance/progress and cross-database failure matrix.
